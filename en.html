Is Hydra Animes free?

Yes. Downloading the APK and watching through the app have no cost. Optional support features exist inside the app, but they are not required to use the player, the catalog or downloads.

Do I need an account?

No, not to browse and watch. Signing in is used to keep favorites and sync progress with AniList or MyAnimeList.

Does it work on Android TV?

Yes, it is the same APK used on phones. On TV, Hydra Animes appears in the app list with its own banner and works with the remote control.

Which audio and subtitle languages are available?

The catalog has subbed and dubbed titles in Brazilian Portuguese, plus English and Latin Spanish episodes when the source provides those tracks. Options are shown in the player, per episode.

Can I watch offline?

Yes. Download an episode or a season from the downloads screen and watch it offline — useful for trips and unstable connections.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest APK on this page and install it over the current version: favorites and progress are kept. The app also notifies you when a new version is available.

Why does Android warn about an unknown source?

Because the APK is distributed outside the Play Store. Only allow the install for the browser or file manager you used, and revoke the permission afterwards if you prefer.

How do I know the APK is the official one?

Download it only from this page: the button goes to this project's Releases. If the file reached you through another site or a message, it is not one of our distributions.
